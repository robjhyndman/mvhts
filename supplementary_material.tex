\section*{Application: results by horizon}

Table~\ref{tab:supp-horizon-arima} breaks down the comparison of joint and separate reconciliation (ARIMA base forecasts) by forecast horizon. For admissions the ratio stays close to one at all horizons. For dismissals, joint reconciliation is worse at short horizons and better at long ones, especially at the national level, so the near-neutral averages in the main text combine gains and losses.
